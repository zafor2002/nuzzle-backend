\section{Introduction}
PetSocial is a social networking web application built specifically for pet owners and
their pets. Where general-purpose social networks treat a pet as an afterthought (a tag,
a photo caption), PetSocial treats each pet as a first-class citizen of the platform: a
pet can have its own profile, its own followers, and its own posts, independent of --- but
linked to --- its owner's account.

\section{Motivation}
General social networks are not designed around the specific needs of pet owners:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item There is no dedicated place to track a pet's health history (vaccinations, vet
        visits, medication) alongside its social presence.
  \item Lost-and-found alerts, breed-specific communities, adoption listings, and
        veterinary booking are all handled by separate, disconnected apps and services.
  \item Owners who wish to keep their identity private (for safety or personal
        preference) have no first-class way to do so while still participating socially
        through their pet's profile.
\end{itemize}

PetSocial addresses these gaps by combining social networking, pet health record
keeping, local community discovery, and pet-commerce (adoption and marketplace) features
into a single, coherent product.

\section{Target Audience}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Individual pet owners who want to share their pet's life and connect with other
        owners.
  \item Breed- and species-specific enthusiast communities.
  \item Animal shelters and rescue organisations posting adoptable pets.
  \item Local veterinary clinics offering appointment booking.
  \item Owners buying and selling pet products peer-to-peer.
\end{itemize}

\section{Product Pillars}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Pet-first identity.} A pet profile is a distinct entity from its owner's
        profile, with its own avatar, bio, species, breed, and follower graph.
  \item \textbf{Privacy by design.} Both an owner account and an individual pet profile
        can independently be marked anonymous, hiding the owner's real name and avatar
        from public view while keeping the account fully functional (moderation and
        messaging still resolve to the real account internally).
  \item \textbf{Utility beyond the feed.} Health logs, lost-and-found alerts, adoption
        listings, veterinary booking, and a marketplace are built directly into the
        product rather than left to third-party apps.
  \item \textbf{Community structure.} Posts and pets are organised into breed- and
        species-based communities, discoverable independently of the follow graph.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Feature Summary}
The table below summarises the major functional areas implemented in the current
version of the product. Each is described in full in the companion
\emph{Feature Specification} document.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{4.5cm}p{9.5cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Area} & \textbf{Summary} \\
\midrule
\endhead
Authentication & Email/password sign-up and login via Supabase Auth, with optional
  email confirmation. \\
Pet \& owner profiles & Independent profiles for pets and owners, each editable, each
  with an avatar upload and an anonymity toggle. \\
Posts \& feed & Photo posts authored as the owner or as one of their pets, with likes
  and comments, and a follow-scoped home feed. \\
Follow graph & Owners follow other owners; the home feed and notifications are scoped
  to this graph. \\
Communities & Automatic grouping of pets and posts by species and breed. \\
Lost \& Found & Owners can report a lost or found pet with a description, optional
  photo association, and contact details. \\
Health logs & Private, owner-only medical history per pet (vaccinations, vet visits,
  medication, reminders). \\
Notifications & In-app notifications for likes, comments, and new followers. \\
Direct messages & One-to-one conversations between owners, integrated as the contact
  method for adoption and marketplace listings. \\
Pet adoption & Shelters and individual owners can list adoptable pets, browsable and
  filterable by species. \\
Veterinary booking & A directory of veterinary clinics with real slot-based appointment
  booking (no double-booking, no booking in the past). \\
Marketplace & Peer-to-peer buying and selling of pet products, filterable by category. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
