\section{Overview}
PetSocial uses a single PostgreSQL database, accessed exclusively through the Prisma
ORM. The schema lives in \texttt{prisma/schema.prisma} and is the single source of
truth for the data model; every table in the database was created by an applied Prisma
migration under \texttt{prisma/migrations/}. There are sixteen models in total, grouped
below by functional area.

\section{Identity}

\subsection{Owner}
Represents a human user account. Its primary key is the same UUID that Supabase Auth
assigns to the corresponding row in \texttt{auth.users}, so an \texttt{Owner} row is
created immediately after a successful sign-up and is never independently generated.

\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & Matches the Supabase Auth user id. \\
email & String & Unique. \\
username & String & Unique; used in profile URLs. \\
displayName & String & Public name, hidden from display when \texttt{isAnonymous} is
  true. \\
avatarUrl & String? & Public URL of the uploaded avatar image, or null for an
  initials-based placeholder. \\
bio & String? & Free-text biography. \\
isAnonymous & Boolean & Hides the owner's real name and avatar everywhere in the
  product; the underlying account is unaffected for moderation and messaging purposes.
  Default \texttt{false}. \\
isPrivate & Boolean & Reserved for a future private-account feature; not yet enforced
  anywhere in the application. Default \texttt{false}. \\
createdAt / updatedAt & DateTime & Standard timestamps. \\
}

\subsection{Pet}
Represents an individual pet. A pet belongs to exactly one owner but is a distinct
public identity: it has its own name, species, breed, avatar, bio, and posts, and its
own independent anonymity flag.

\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
name & String & \\
species & String & Free text (e.g.\ ``Dog'', ``Cat''); used to group pets into
  communities. \\
breed & String? & Free text; used as a secondary filter within a species community. \\
bio & String? & \\
birthDate & DateTime? & Not currently surfaced in any page; reserved for a future age
  display. \\
avatarUrl & String? & \\
isAnonymous & Boolean & Hides the \emph{owner's} identity wherever this specific pet is
  shown, independent of the owner's own \texttt{isAnonymous} flag. Default
  \texttt{false}. \\
createdAt / updatedAt & DateTime & \\
}

\paragraph{Anonymity resolution.} Whether an owner's identity is hidden next to a given
pet or post is computed as
$\texttt{isHidden} = \texttt{pet.isAnonymous} \lor \texttt{owner.isAnonymous}$,
implemented once in \texttt{src/lib/visibility.ts} and reused by every page that
displays authorship.

\section{Social Graph and Content}

\subsection{Post}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & The authoring account. Cascade delete. \\
petId & String? (FK $\to$ Pet) & Optional; set when the post is authored ``as'' one of
  the owner's pets. On pet deletion the field is set to null (\texttt{SetNull}), not
  cascaded, so the post survives. \\
caption & String? & \\
mediaUrls & String[] & Currently holds at most one URL in practice (the UI only
  supports a single photo per post), stored as an array for forward compatibility. \\
createdAt / updatedAt & DateTime & \\
}

\subsection{Comment}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
postId & String (FK $\to$ Post) & Cascade delete. \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
body & String & \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}

\subsection{Like}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
postId & String (FK $\to$ Post) & Cascade delete. \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}
A unique constraint on \texttt{(postId, ownerId)} enforces at most one like per owner
per post at the database level, independent of any application-level check.

\subsection{Follow}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
followerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & The owner doing the following. \\
followingId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & The owner being followed. \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}
A unique constraint on \texttt{(followerId, followingId)} prevents duplicate follow
edges. The home feed query filters posts to
\texttt{ownerId IN (currentUser, ...following)}.

\subsection{Hashtag and HashtagsOnPosts}
A conventional many-to-many join between \texttt{Post} and a \texttt{Hashtag} table
(\texttt{tag} is unique). \textbf{These two models are defined in the schema but not
yet used by any page or Server Action} --- no code currently extracts hashtags from a
caption or writes to either table. They are documented here for completeness and as a
known gap; see the Feature Specification document, \S~``Not Yet Implemented''.

\subsection{Message}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
senderId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & \\
receiverId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & \\
body & String & \\
readAt & DateTime? & Null until the receiver opens the conversation, at which point it
  is set for every unread message from that sender in a single \texttt{updateMany}. \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}

\subsection{Notification}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & The recipient. \\
type & String & One of \texttt{"like"}, \texttt{"comment"}, \texttt{"follow"}. \\
message & String & Pre-rendered human-readable text, e.g.\ ``Alice liked your post.'' \\
isRead & Boolean & Default \texttt{false}. \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}
Notifications are never generated for a user's own actions on their own content (an
owner liking their own post does not notify them).

\section{Pet Care}

\subsection{HealthLog}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
petId & String (FK $\to$ Pet) & Cascade delete. \\
type & String & One of \texttt{"vaccination"}, \texttt{"vet\_visit"},
  \texttt{"medication"}, \texttt{"other"}. \\
title & String & \\
notes & String? & \\
date & DateTime & The date of the recorded event. \\
reminderAt & DateTime? & Optional follow-up reminder date. \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}
Health logs are only ever queried when the viewer is the pet's own owner; the query
itself is gated (\S~``Owner-Only Data'' in the Security and Privacy document), not just
hidden in the UI.

\subsection{LostFoundPost}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
petId & String? (FK $\to$ Pet) & Optional link to one of the owner's own pets. \\
status & String & \texttt{"lost"} or \texttt{"found"}. Default \texttt{"lost"}. \\
description & String & \\
lastSeenLat / lastSeenLng & Float? & Reserved for a future map view; not currently
  collected by the report form. \\
contactInfo & String? & \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
resolvedAt & DateTime? & Set when the owner marks the report resolved; resolved
  reports are excluded from the active listing. \\
}

\subsection{Vet}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
name & String & Veterinarian's name. \\
clinicName & String & \\
location & String? & Free-text distance/address string. \\
bio & String? & \\
avatarUrl & String? & \\
rating & Float & Default \texttt{4.8}. \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}
The table self-seeds with three demonstration clinics the first time
\texttt{/vet} is loaded and finds it empty (\texttt{src/lib/vet-seed.ts}); there is no
vet sign-up flow.

\subsection{Appointment}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
ownerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
petId & String (FK $\to$ Pet) & Cascade delete. \\
vetId & String (FK $\to$ Vet) & Cascade delete. \\
reason & String & Free text, chosen from a fixed list in the booking form. \\
scheduledAt & DateTime & The exact appointment instant. \\
status & String & \texttt{"confirmed"}, \texttt{"cancelled"}, or \texttt{"completed"}
  (the last is defined but never set by any current code path). Default
  \texttt{"confirmed"}. \\
notes & String? & \\
createdAt & DateTime & \\
}
Availability is computed, not stored: a fixed list of eight daily time slots is
filtered against existing \texttt{"confirmed"} appointments for the same vet and date,
and against the current time (already-passed slots on the current day are excluded).

\section{Commerce}

\subsection{AdoptionListing}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
posterId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
name & String & The pet's name. \\
species / breed / age / gender & String / String? / String? / String? & \texttt{species}
  is required; the rest are optional free text. \\
description & String & \\
mediaUrls & String[] & At most one photo in practice. \\
location / contactInfo & String? / String? & \\
status & String & \texttt{"available"}, \texttt{"pending"}, or \texttt{"adopted"}.
  Default \texttt{"available"}. \\
createdAt / updatedAt & DateTime & \\
}
The browse page excludes \texttt{"adopted"} listings from the active list entirely
(they remain in the database and reachable by direct link, but do not appear in
\texttt{/adoption}).

\subsection{MarketplaceListing}
\modeltable{
id & String (PK) & \\
sellerId & String (FK $\to$ Owner) & Cascade delete. \\
title & String & \\
category & String & One of \texttt{"food"}, \texttt{"toys"}, \texttt{"beds"},
  \texttt{"health"}, \texttt{"accessories"}, \texttt{"other"}. \\
price & Float & In whole currency units (e.g.\ dollars), not cents. \\
description & String & \\
mediaUrls & String[] & \\
location & String? & \\
status & String & \texttt{"available"} or \texttt{"sold"}. Default
  \texttt{"available"}. \\
createdAt / updatedAt & DateTime & \\
}
Unlike an adopted pet, a sold marketplace listing is \emph{not} hidden from
\texttt{/marketplace}: it remains visible with a ``Sold'' badge, since a marketplace
listing's history is treated as useful browsing context rather than something to hide.
