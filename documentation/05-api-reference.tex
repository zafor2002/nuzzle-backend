\section{Conventions}
PetSocial has no separate REST or GraphQL API; all mutations are Next.js
\textbf{Server Actions} --- plain async functions marked \texttt{"use server"} and
called either as a form's \texttt{action} or directly from a Client Component inside
\texttt{useTransition}. Every action documented below follows the same shape:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Resolve and require the current user via \texttt{requireUser()}, which
        redirects to \texttt{/login} if there is no session.
  \item Check a per-user rate limit via \texttt{rateLimit(key, limit, windowMs)}.
  \item Parse and validate input with a Zod schema built from the submitted
        \texttt{FormData}.
  \item Perform authorisation checks specific to the action (e.g.\ ``this pet belongs
        to the caller'').
  \item Apply the change via Prisma, then call \texttt{revalidatePath()} for every
        route the change affects.
\end{enumerate}

Actions that create a new resource end with \texttt{redirect()} to that resource's
page. Actions invoked from a button (likes, follows, status toggles) instead return an
optional \texttt{\{ error: string \}} and rely on \texttt{revalidatePath} plus Next.js's
automatic action-triggered refresh to update the calling page.

The one route handler in the application that is \emph{not} a Server Action is
\texttt{src/app/auth/callback/route.ts}, a conventional Next.js Route Handler that
exchanges a Supabase email-confirmation code for a session.

\section{Authentication --- \texttt{actions/auth.ts}}

\subsection{signup(formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 5 per hour, keyed by client IP. \\
Validation & \texttt{email} (valid email), \texttt{password} (6--72 chars),
  \texttt{username} (3--30 chars, lowercase letters/digits/underscore only),
  \texttt{displayName} (1--100 chars). \\
Behaviour & Rejects if the username is already taken. Calls Supabase
  \texttt{auth.signUp()}, then creates the corresponding \texttt{Owner} row using the
  new Supabase user id as its primary key. Redirects to \texttt{/signup/check-email} if
  no session was returned (email confirmation required) or to \texttt{/} otherwise. \\
}

\subsection{login(formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 8 per 5 minutes, keyed by the submitted email. \\
Validation & \texttt{email} (valid email), \texttt{password} (non-empty, up to 72
  chars). \\
Behaviour & Calls Supabase \texttt{auth.signInWithPassword()}; redirects to \texttt{/}
  on success. \\
}

\subsection{logout()}
\actiontable{
Behaviour & Calls Supabase \texttt{auth.signOut()} and redirects to \texttt{/login}. \\
}

\section{Profile Management --- \texttt{actions/profile.ts}}

\subsection{updateOwnerProfile(formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 20 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{displayName} (1--100 chars), \texttt{bio} (up to 500 chars,
  optional), \texttt{avatarUrl} (valid URL, optional), \texttt{isAnonymous}
  (checkbox). \\
Behaviour & Updates the caller's own \texttt{Owner} row. \texttt{avatarUrl} is only
  written if a new upload occurred (an empty value leaves the existing avatar
  untouched). Redirects to the owner's own profile. \\
}

\subsection{updatePet(petId, formData)}
\actiontable{
Auth & Verifies \texttt{petId} belongs to the caller before any validation runs. \\
Rate limit & 30 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{name}/\texttt{species} (1--60 chars), \texttt{breed} (up to 60
  chars, optional), \texttt{bio} (up to 500 chars, optional), \texttt{avatarUrl} (valid
  URL, optional), \texttt{isAnonymous} (checkbox). \\
Behaviour & Updates the pet row; redirects to the pet's profile. \\
}

\section{Pets --- \texttt{actions/pets.ts}}

\subsection{createPet(formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 10 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{name}/\texttt{species} (1--60 chars), \texttt{breed} (up to 60
  chars, optional), \texttt{bio} (up to 500 chars, optional), \texttt{isAnonymous}
  (checkbox). \\
Behaviour & Creates a \texttt{Pet} owned by the caller; redirects to its profile page. \\
}

\section{Posts --- \texttt{actions/posts.ts}}

\subsection{createPost(formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 20 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & At least one of \texttt{caption} (up to 2000 chars) or \texttt{mediaUrl}
  (valid URL) must be present; \texttt{petId} optional. \\
Behaviour & If \texttt{petId} is supplied, verifies it belongs to the caller before
  creating the post. Redirects to \texttt{/feed}. \\
}

\section{Engagement --- \texttt{actions/engagement.ts}}

\subsection{toggleLike(postId)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 60 per minute, keyed by user id. \\
Behaviour & Toggles a \texttt{Like} row for the caller on the given post (the unique
  \texttt{(postId, ownerId)} database constraint makes double-liking impossible even
  under a race). Sends a ``liked your post'' notification to the post's owner on the
  like transition only, and never when the caller is the post's own owner. \\
}

\subsection{addComment(postId, formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 20 per minute, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{body}, 1--1000 chars. \\
Behaviour & Creates a \texttt{Comment}; sends a ``commented on your post'' notification
  to the post's owner (never to self). \\
}

\section{Follows --- \texttt{actions/follows.ts}}

\subsection{toggleFollow(targetOwnerId)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 60 per minute, keyed by user id. \\
Behaviour & No-ops if \texttt{targetOwnerId} equals the caller's own id. Toggles a
  \texttt{Follow} edge (unique on \texttt{(followerId, followingId)}); sends a
  ``started following you'' notification on the follow transition only. \\
}

\section{Health Logs --- \texttt{actions/health.ts}}

\subsection{createHealthLog(petId, formData)}
\actiontable{
Auth & Verifies \texttt{petId} belongs to the caller. \\
Rate limit & 30 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{type} (one of vaccination / vet\_visit / medication / other),
  \texttt{title} (1--120 chars), \texttt{notes} (up to 1000 chars, optional),
  \texttt{date} (coerced to a Date), \texttt{reminderAt} (coerced to a Date,
  optional). \\
Behaviour & Creates a \texttt{HealthLog}; redirects to the pet's profile. \\
}

\section{Lost \& Found --- \texttt{actions/lost-found.ts}}

\subsection{createLostFoundPost(formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 10 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{status} (lost / found), \texttt{petId} (optional, verified to
  belong to the caller if present), \texttt{description} (1--2000 chars),
  \texttt{contactInfo} (up to 200 chars, optional). \\
Behaviour & Creates a \texttt{LostFoundPost}; redirects to \texttt{/lost-found}. \\
}

\subsection{markResolved(id)}
\actiontable{
Auth & Verifies the report belongs to the caller (silently no-ops otherwise). \\
Behaviour & Sets \texttt{resolvedAt} to now, removing the report from the active
  listing. \\
}

\subsection{markAllRead()}

}

\section{Messages --- \texttt{actions/messages.ts}}

\subsection{sendMessage(receiverId, formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 40 per minute, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{body}, 1--1000 chars; rejects sending to oneself; rejects if
  \texttt{receiverId} does not correspond to an existing owner. \\
Behaviour & Creates a \texttt{Message}. Marking messages read happens separately, as a

}

\section{Veterinary Booking --- \texttt{actions/vet.ts}}

\subsection{bookAppointment(vetId, formData)}
\actiontable{
Rate limit & 10 per hour, keyed by user id. \\
Validation & \texttt{petId} (non-empty, verified to belong to the caller),
  \texttt{reason} (1--120 chars), \texttt{notes} (up to 1000, optional), \texttt{date}
  (\texttt{YYYY-MM-DD}), \texttt{time} (\texttt{HH:MM}). \\
Behaviour & Verifies the vet exists, that the combined date/time is in the future, and
  that no \texttt{"confirmed"} appointment already occupies that exact vet/time slot,
  before creating the \texttt{Appointment}. Redirects to
  \texttt{/vet/appointments}. \\
}

\subsection{cancelAppointment(id)}
\actiontable{
Auth & Verifies the appointment belongs to the caller. \\
Behaviour & Sets \texttt{status = "cancelled"}, freeing the slot for other bookings. \\
}

\section{Route Handler --- \texttt{app/auth/callback/route.ts}}
\actiontable{
Method & \texttt{GET} \\
Purpose & Exchanges the \texttt{code} query parameter (from a Supabase email
  confirmation link) for a session via \texttt{exchangeCodeForSession()}, then redirects
  to the \texttt{next} query parameter or \texttt{/} on success, or to \texttt{/login}
  on failure. \\
}
